# Area Phrase Inventory

## Purpose

This document lists the phrases the learner most likely needs to say and understand in each major area of the app.

Phrase selection is based on:

- the current trip itinerary
- JNTO travel phrase guidance and handbook
- practical beginner Japanese needed to complete the likely tasks

Some phrase choices below are direct travel necessities. Others are inferred from the itinerary and grouped because they are high-probability interactions.

## Format

Each area has:

- `Need to Say`: phrases the learner should actively produce
- `Need to Understand`: phrases the learner is likely to hear from staff or signs

Romanization is included here because the app is still planning for a zero-experience learner.

## Airport

### Need to Say

- `Sumimasen.` - Excuse me.
- `Check-in o onegaishimasu.` - Check-in, please.
- `Kono pasupooto de ii desu ka?` - Is this passport okay?
- `Kore de daijoubu desu ka?` - Is this okay?
- `Haneda e wa doko desu ka?` - Where is Haneda?
- `Nanji desu ka?` - What time is it?
- `Kono densha / basu wa ... ni ikimasu ka?` - Does this train / bus go to ... ?
- `Mou ichido onegaishimasu.` - One more time, please.

### Need to Understand

- `Pasupooto o misete kudasai.` - Please show me your passport.
- `Koko ni narande kudasai.` - Please line up here.
- `Tsugi no kata douzo.` - Next person, please.
- `Kochira e douzo.` - This way, please.
- `Go-jousha / go-joujou kudasai.` - Please board.
- `... ban geeto desu.` - It is gate number ...
- `Enchou / okurete imasu.` - It is delayed.

## Hotel and Check-In

### Need to Say

- `Yoyaku shite arimasu. [name] desu.` - I have a reservation. My name is ...
- `Check-in o onegaishimasu.` - Check-in, please.
- `Wi-Fi wa tsukaemasu ka?` - Can I use Wi-Fi?
- `Takushii o yonde moraemasu ka?` - Could you call a taxi for me?
- `Nimotsu o azukatte kudasai.` - Please hold my luggage.
- `Kurejitto kaado de haraemasu ka?` - Can I pay by credit card?
- `Kono atari ni osusume no resutoran wa arimasu ka?` - Is there a restaurant you recommend around here?

### Need to Understand

- `Pasupooto o misete itadakemasu ka?` - May I see your passport?
- `Choushoku-tsuki desu.` - Breakfast is included.
- `Manshitsu desu.` - We are full.
- `Asa shoku wa ... ji kara desu.` - Breakfast is from ... o'clock.
- `Kagi wa kochira desu.` - Here is your key.

## Convenience Store

### Need to Say

- `Kore o onegaishimasu.` - This one, please.
- `Futatsu onegaishimasu.` - Two, please.
- `Atatamete kudasai.` - Please heat it up.
- `Fukuro wa daijoubu desu.` - I do not need a bag.
- `Kaado wa tsukaemasu ka?` - Can I use a card?
- `Toire wa doko desu ka?` - Where is the restroom?

### Need to Understand

- `Atatamemasu ka?` - Shall I heat it up?
- `Fukuro wa irimasu ka?` - Do you need a bag?
- `Rejibukuro wa ...` - bag-related cashier phrasing
- `Pointo kaado wa omochi desu ka?` - Do you have a points card?
- `Otsuri desu.` - Here is your change.

## Trains and Stations

### Need to Say

- `... ni iku ni wa dono densha ni noreba ii desu ka?` - Which train should I take to go to ... ?
- `Doko de orireba ii desu ka?` - Where should I get off?
- `Kippu wa doko de kaemasu ka?` - Where can I buy a ticket?
- `Ichinichi joushaken wa arimasu ka?` - Do you have a one-day pass?
- `Kore de noremasu ka?` - Can I ride with this?
- `Shiteiseki o onegaishimasu.` - Reserved seat, please.
- `Nan-ban sen desu ka?` - Which platform is it?

### Need to Understand

- `... ban noriba desu.` - It is platform number ...
- `... de norikaete kudasai.` - Please transfer at ...
- `Tsugi wa ... desu.` - The next stop is ...
- `Kaisoku / futsuu / tokkyuu ...` - rapid / local / limited express service labels
- `Densha ga okurete imasu.` - The train is delayed.

## Directions and Street Navigation

### Need to Say

- `... wa doko desu ka?` - Where is ... ?
- `Koko wa doko desu ka?` - Where am I?
- `Mappu de oshiete kudasai.` - Please show me on the map.
- `Massugu desu ka?` - Is it straight ahead?
- `Chikai desu ka?` - Is it near?

### Need to Understand

- `Massugu itte kudasai.` - Go straight.
- `Migi / hidari ni magatte kudasai.` - Turn right / left.
- `Sugu soko desu.` - It is right there.
- `Aruite ... fun desu.` - It is ... minutes on foot.

## Restaurant and Cafe

### Need to Say

- `Eigo no menyuu wa arimasu ka?` - Do you have an English menu?
- `Kore o onegaishimasu.` - This, please.
- `Ano hito to onaji no o kudasai.` - The same as that person, please.
- `... nuki de onegaishimasu.` - Without ..., please.
- `Suupu-n / fooku o kudasai.` - Spoon / fork, please.
- `Okaikei onegaishimasu.` - Check, please.
- `Oishii desu.` - It is delicious.

### Need to Understand

- `Go-chuumon wa?` - What would you like to order?
- `Nomimono wa?` - What would you like to drink?
- `Kinen / kin-en seki desu.` - smoking / non-smoking seat wording
- `Osusume wa ... desu.` - The recommendation is ...
- `Okaikei wa kochira desu.` - Please pay here.

## Market and Street Food

### Need to Say

- `Hitotsu onegaishimasu.` - One, please.
- `Kore wa nan desu ka?` - What is this?
- `Osusume wa nan desu ka?` - What do you recommend?
- `Tabearuki dekimasu ka?` - Can I eat while walking? / more likely etiquette understanding, but useful
- `Motte kaeremasu ka?` - Can I take it to go?

### Need to Understand

- `Atsui node ki o tsukete kudasai.` - It is hot, be careful.
- `Koko de tabete kudasai.` - Please eat here.
- `Aruki-nagara tabenaide kudasai.` - Please do not eat while walking.
- `Genkin nomi desu.` - Cash only.

## Shopping and Souvenirs

### Need to Say

- `Koko de ... wa utte imasu ka?` - Do you sell ... here?
- `Motto ookii / chiisai no wa arimasu ka?` - Do you have a bigger / smaller one?
- `Hoka no iro wa arimasu ka?` - Do you have another color?
- `Kore o shichaku dekimasu ka?` - Can I try this on?
- `Nihon-sei desu ka?` - Is this made in Japan?
- `Menzei de kaemasu ka?` - Can I buy this duty-free?
- `Kore o kaigai ni okuremasu ka?` - Can you send this overseas?

### Need to Understand

- `Shichakushitsu wa kochira desu.` - The fitting room is here.
- `Saizu wa ... desu.` - The size is ...
- `Kono iro nara arimasu.` - We have it in this color.
- `Menzei no taishou desu.` - It qualifies for tax-free purchase.

## Shrine and Temple

### Need to Say

- `Koko ni haitte mo ii desu ka?` - May I enter here?
- `Shashin o totte mo ii desu ka?` - May I take photos?
- `Omamori wa doko desu ka?` - Where are the charms?
- `Goshuin wa arimasu ka?` - Do you offer goshuin?

### Need to Understand

- `Koko wa kinshi desu.` - This area is prohibited.
- `Shashin kinshi desu.` - No photos.
- `Kutsu o nuide kudasai.` - Please remove your shoes.
- `Shizuka ni onegaishimasu.` - Please be quiet.

## Ryokan and Onsen

### Need to Say

- `Yoyaku shite arimasu.` - I have a reservation.
- `Shokuji wa doko / nanji desu ka?` - Where / what time is the meal?
- `Ofuro wa nanji made desu ka?` - Until what time is the bath open?
- `Heya de taberaremasu ka?` - Can I eat in my room?
- `Yukata wa doko desu ka?` - Where is the yukata?

### Need to Understand

- `Oshokuji wa ... ji desu.` - The meal is at ... o'clock.
- `Kashikiri buro` or `private onsen` timing wording
- `Tattoos ...` policy wording
- `Go-jiyuu ni douzo.` - Please help yourself.
- `Koko de ashi o aratte kudasai.` - Please wash here first.

## Pharmacy and Help

### Need to Say

- `Kusuri wa arimasu ka?` - Do you have medicine?
- `Atama ga itai desu.` - I have a headache.
- `Nodo ga itai desu.` - My throat hurts.
- `Netsu ga arimasu.` - I have a fever.
- `Onaka ga itai desu.` - My stomach hurts.

### Need to Understand

- `Doko ga itai desu ka?` - Where does it hurt?
- `Kore o tabeta ato ni nonde kudasai.` - Take this after food.
- `Ichinichi ... kai desu.` - ... times a day.

## Phrase Selection Notes

Not every phrase above should be taught in the same way.

Some phrases belong in:

- foundation tracks, because they transfer across many areas
- area-specific tracks, because they only make sense in context

That mapping is captured in `curriculum-synthesis.md`.
