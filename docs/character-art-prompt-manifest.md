# Character Art Prompt Manifest

## Purpose

This document keeps right-side character art generation consistent across locations.

Each location should have:

- one primary guide character
- a clear setting
- readable cultural cues
- a mood that matches the location

The goal is visual identity, not generic anime portraits.

## Global Art Direction

- warm, premium, polished anime illustration
- friendly and inviting expression
- strong environmental storytelling
- character positioned so the lower portion can tolerate text overlay
- enough negative space around the body for UI flexibility
- portrait should feel like a host or guide, not a pin-up
- avoid exaggerated fantasy styling

## Output Requirements

- portrait orientation
- high resolution suitable for iPad
- clean enough contrast for white overlay text
- consistent quality across locations

## Restaurant

Folder:

- `assets/characters/locations/restaurant/`

Prompt intent:

- young Japanese restaurant hostess or server
- welcoming smile
- warm restaurant or izakaya-style evening setting
- subtle menu or lantern details in the background
- clothing that feels authentic and practical, not costume-like
- enough dark background contrast for white dialogue overlay

Prompt draft:

`A premium anime-style illustration of a friendly young Japanese restaurant guide standing in a warmly lit traditional-modern restaurant at night, subtle lantern light, wood textures, menu boards and cozy dining atmosphere in the background, natural welcoming smile, practical elegant service attire inspired by Japan hospitality, cinematic lighting, highly detailed face and environment, portrait composition, space near lower body for UI text overlay, polished and believable, not cosplay, not exaggerated fantasy`
