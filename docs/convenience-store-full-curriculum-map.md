# Convenience Store Full Curriculum Map

This document defines the first full authored pass for the `Convenience Store` category in `Tokyo`.

The point of this branch is to make the most common first-night and in-between errand language feel normal:

- understand the store layout
- recognize common snacks, drinks, and quick meals
- ask where something is
- survive checkout language
- handle bag, heating, and receipt questions

## Category Structure

`Tokyo -> City Systems -> Conbini Basics`

Current subcategory:

- `Conbini Basics`

## Conbini Basics

Purpose:
- survive a normal Japanese convenience-store visit without relying entirely on guessing

Internal lesson levels:
- `Store Words and Signs`
- `Drinks and Snacks`
- `Meals and Hot Foods`
- `Finding Items`
- `Checkout Basics`
- `Bag, Heat, and Receipt`
- `Shelf Categories and Signs`
- `Desserts and Extra Drinks`
- `Breakfast and Light Foods`
- `Payment and Small Responses`
- `Common Clerk Questions`
- `Mixed Conbini Review`

What it teaches:
- entrance, exit, register, drinks
- tea, water, coffee, rice balls, snacks
- bento, sandwich, ramen, fried chicken, would you like it heated
- excuse me, where is it, do you have it, where are the rice balls, do you have water
- bag, receipt, cash, please
- do you need a bag, no thanks, yes please, receipt please, please heat it
- frozen foods, sweets, bread, cup noodles, daily goods
- juice, soda, ice cream, pudding, dessert
- yogurt, milk, salad, fruit, salad chicken
- card, credit card, points, yes, no
- do you need the receipt, would you like chopsticks, do you have a point card
- mixed review across store, shelf, food, and checkout language

## Authoring Notes

This branch is intentionally practical and high-frequency.

It does not try to teach:

- every food item in a convenience store
- advanced payment troubleshooting
- every promotional sign or store announcement

Those can become later lesson levels or a deeper expansion.

The success condition is:

- the learner can walk in, find a few basics, choose simple food and drinks, and get through checkout naturally.
